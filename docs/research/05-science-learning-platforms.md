# 05 — Online science learning platforms audited against CCEA GCSE Double Award Science

**Date:** 1 September 2026
**Scope:** Platforms and resources a Northern Ireland student sitting **CCEA GCSE Science: Double Award (2017 spec, G9824)** might use, and how well each maps to the seven CCEA units (B1, B2, C1, C2, P1, P2, Unit 7 Practical Skills) and the A*–G double-grade system.
**Method:** Web search (200-search session budget was exhausted mid-task), direct fetches, and — for sites that block automated fetching (bbc.co.uk, ccea.org.uk, savemyexams.com, phet.colorado.edu licensing page, isaacscience.org, help.quizlet.com, educake.co.uk) — the Claude browser pane. Every URL below was either fetched/rendered in this session or is a search-result URL explicitly labelled as such. Pages that could not be loaded are flagged.

### 1.3 Observations on the Bitesize Double Award pathway

- Roughly 100 pages in total (≈24 biology, 18 chemistry, 35 physics topic articles + 18 prescribed-practical pages + practical-skills pages). Topic headings use CCEA's own unit titles for chemistry.
- Compared with the CCEA **single-science** hubs, the Double Award pathway is thinner: no "Cells and specialisation", "Diffusion", "Hormones" or "Global warming" articles (all present on the CCEA Biology hub); no "Solubility" (chemistry); no "Heat transfer", "Reflection and refraction of waves", "Critical angle/TIR", "Motor effect", "AC generator", "Transformer" or "Space travel" articles (all present on the CCEA Physics hub). Whether each omission is spec-correct for Double Award needs checking against the CCEA spec, but it means students often have to hop to the single-science pages.
- The "exam-style quiz by topic" and "exam-style questions" pages are **generic Combined Science quizzes organised by AQA paper structure** ("Biology Paper 1: Cell biology; Organisation…"); the page says they suit "any awarding body". They do **not** follow B1/B2 etc.
- Only 6 of the 18 prescribed practicals per science? No — Bitesize lists 6 biology, 6 chemistry and 6 physics practicals, i.e. all 18 for Double Award. The single-science hubs list 9 per science.
- Interactivity is limited to a few in-page quizzes and one "States of matter interactive activity". No progress tracking, no login.
- **Licence/embed:** BBC content is BBC copyright; there is no embed facility. Link out only. CCEA past papers on Bitesize are "linked to with their permission" — a platform hosting the PDFs itself would need CCEA's permission.

### 2.2 Free Science Lessons (freesciencelessons.co.uk / YouTube)
- Offers: video collections for GCSE Biology/Chemistry/Physics (two papers each) and A level; printed workbooks via Amazon; downloadable workbooks; "Plus" courses at £8.50 each, 10% off multiples. https://www.freesciencelessons.co.uk/

- Embed: standard YouTube embed; FAQ says nothing about reuse rights.

## 3. Mismatch analysis — what an AQA/Edexcel platform gets wrong for a CCEA Double Award student

| Dimension | AQA/Edexcel-centric platforms | CCEA Double Award reality | Consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Structure | "Paper 1 / Paper 2" per science, Combined Science Trilogy topic order | Six unit exams (B1, C1, P1 in Year 11; B2, C2, P2 in Year 12) + Unit 7 | Wrong revision sequencing; students revise Year-12 content for Year-11 unit exams |
| Grades | 9-1, Combined Science double 9-9 → 1-1 | A*–G double grades (A*A*, A*A, AA, AB, BB, BC*, C*C*, C*C, CC … GG) including the C* grade; UMS 600 | Grade calculators and target-setting tools are meaningless |
| Practicals | AQA "required practicals" (assessed inside written papers) | 18 **prescribed practicals**, plus a separately examined **Practical Skills unit worth 25%** (Booklet A performed, Booklet B written) | Nothing on mainstream platforms trains Booklet A/B; Malmesbury/Cognito practical videos cover a different list |
| Content | AQA-specific items (e.g. "Chemistry of the atmosphere", "Using resources") | CCEA-specific items (e.g. C1 Solubility & analysis, C2 Gas chemistry, P1 Moments & Kinetic theory, P2 Space physics, B1 Nitrogen cycle) | Over-revision of non-examined content, under-revision of CCEA topics |
| Tiering flags | 9-1 Higher-only marked differently | CCEA Higher-only statements (e.g. TIR/critical angle, motor effect) | Foundation students waste time; Higher students miss content |
| Entry timing | Single summer sitting | November/March series for Unit 1s (CCEA past papers page shows March 2026 and November series) | No planner supports unit re-sits |
| Terminology | "Combined Science" | "Double Award" / "Single Award"; Irish-medium versions exist | Search discoverability and labelling |

## 4. Gaps in the market for a CCEA Double Award student

1. **No free, individually accessible, CCEA-unit-structured interactive course.** The two CCEA-mapped question banks (Educake, GCSEPod) are school-only subscriptions; the free adaptive platforms (Seneca, Cognito, Tassomai, Isaac, Oak) have no CCEA course.
2. **Unit 7 Practical Skills (25% of the qualification) is unserved** apart from Bitesize's 18 prescribed-practical pages and CCEA's PDF Practical Manual. No interactive planning/analysis trainer, no Booklet-B-style question practice, no auto-marked graph/uncertainty skills.
3. **Video coverage is patchy**: physics is well served (GCSE Physics Online CCEA, PhysicsRocksItsTrue); chemistry has one specimen-paper playlist (Chemistry Chicken); biology has only Science Shorts' whole-paper summaries. Structured DA biology/chemistry video courses exist only as paid bundles (Simply Science, Forkhill waiting list).
4. **No A*–G double-grade / UMS tooling** (600-UMS totals, tier combination outcomes, C* grade). CCEA publishes the numbers as PDFs only.
5. **Bitesize's exam-practice quizzes are AQA-shaped**, and its Double Award pathway is thinner than its CCEA single-science pathways.
6. **No unit-timetable/re-sit planner** for the November/March/Summer series.
7. **Interactive simulations are unlicensed for CCEA topics**: PhET is the obvious source, but its March-2026 move to CC BY-NC means any paid or ad-supported platform needs a commercial licence.
8. **Irish-medium students** have only CCEA PDFs (Comhaid Fíricí, Lámhleabhar Praiticiúil).
9. **Past papers are duplicated everywhere but never turned into topic-tagged question banks** with mark-scheme feedback (Save My Exams, Revision Science, Bitesize all stop at PDF links; CCEA copyright applies).

## 5. Implications for the CCEA GCSE learning platform

1. **Adopt CCEA's unit taxonomy verbatim** (B1 → Unit 7) with tier tags (F/H, Higher-only statements) and Year-11 / Year-12 sequencing; expose CCEA's own topic names so search and teacher trust align.
2. **Build the Unit 7 practical-skills strand as a headline feature**: one interactive module per prescribed practical (method, variables, data handling, graph skills, evaluation), Booklet-A checklists and Booklet-B-style auto-marked questions. This is the largest unserved 25% of the grade.
3. **Simulations**: embed PhET via the verified iframe pattern (see 2.11) *only* if the platform is non-commercial and ad-free; otherwise budget for a PhET commercial licence or build in-house sims. Always render the CC BY-NC attribution line beside each iframe and never obscure the PhET logo.
4. **Link, don't host, BBC and CCEA content**: deep-link to the exact Bitesize article IDs mapped in §1.2 as "read more", and to CCEA past-paper PDFs; seek CCEA permission before hosting or re-publishing papers/mark-scheme text, mirroring the BBC "linked to with their permission" model.
5. **Video**: embed the verified NI-teacher YouTube playlists (Chemistry Chicken, Science Shorts, PhysicsRocksItsTrue) and GCSE Physics Online's free CCEA videos with credit; consider a FuseSchool licence only for hosted/offline use.
6. **Reusable open content**: Oak's OGL-licensed explanations and quiz items can be pulled through the Open API and re-mapped to CCEA topics with attribution; flag AQA-only material during curation.
7. **Flashcards**: embed selected public Quizlet CCEA sets as a stop-gap (logo stays), but plan first-party spaced-repetition decks per unit with CCEA "need-to-know" equations and definitions.
8. **Grade tooling**: implement a CCEA double-grade calculator using the 600-UMS thresholds and the "Grade Outcomes for Double Award Science Tier Combinations" logic, plus a unit-exam/re-sit planner for November/March/Summer series.
9. **Differentiate from Educake/GCSEPod** by being individually accessible (student/parent sign-up) and by exposing CCEA-mark-scheme-style feedback, which neither school platform offers to non-subscribing students.
10. **Irish-medium toggle** for key terms, leveraging CCEA's Comhaid Fíricí as the vocabulary source.
