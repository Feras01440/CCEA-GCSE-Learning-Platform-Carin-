## WORKED EXAMPLES
### we.science.b1.b1-plant-hormones-phototropism.01 [verified] paper B1 calc
STEM: A young shoot is lit from one side only.
Explain how the shoot comes to bend towards the light. [4]
  FIG: svg — A shoot lit from one side, with auxin made at the tip, moving down, collecting on the shaded side, and the cells on that side drawn longer than those on the lit side.
  1. Auxin is produced at the tip of the shoot.   — Start where the specification starts. The first bullet of 1.6.12 is the site of production, and it is the cheapest mark in the question.  (P1)
  2. The auxin moves down the shoot.   — Second bullet. It is easy to skip because it feels obvious, but it is a separate marking point.  (P2)
  3. The one-sided light makes the auxin unevenly distributed, with more on the shaded side.   — Third bullet. Say which side: 'uneven' alone does not show you know the direction, and the direction is what the whole answer turns on.  (P3)
  4. Auxin makes the cells elongate, so the shaded side grows longer than the lit side and the shoot curves towards the light.   — Fourth bullet. The word is elongate. 'Grows more' is usually accepted but 'makes more cells' is not, because that is cell division, not elongation.  (P4)
  FINAL: Auxin is produced at the tip of the shoot and moves down it. The light coming from one side makes the auxin unevenly distributed, so there is more auxin on the shaded side than on the lit side. Auxin causes cells to elongate, so the cells on the shaded side become longer than those on the lit side and the shoot bends towards the light.
  TWIN: A seedling on a windowsill is lit only from the window side. Explain how auxin makes it lean towards the window.  → text accepted=["auxin is made at the tip of the shoot and moves down it, the light makes the auxin uneven so there is more auxin on the shaded side away from the window, auxin makes those cells elongate so the shaded side grows longer and the shoot bends towards the window"] keyWords=[{"any":["tip"],"marks":1,"reject":["root"]},{"any":["moves down","travels down","down the shoot"],"marks":1},{"any":["shaded side","away from the window","uneven"],"marks":1},{"any":["elongate","longer"],"marks":1,"reject":["divide"]}]

## DIAGNOSTICS
### dx.science.b1.b1-plant-hormones-phototropism (pre) [verified]
- 01 (Name the process): A shoot grows towards a lamp placed to one side of it. What is this response called?
    ✔ phototropism — Phototropism: photo- for light, -tropism for a growth response. Summer 2025 Foundation examiners reported photosynthesis offered here instead.
    ✘ photosynthesis [sci.tropism.process-named-photosynthesis] — Photosynthesis is how the plant makes glucose. The word you want shares the first four letters and nothing else: phototropism.
    ✘ transpiration [sci.tropism.process-named-photosynthesis] — Transpiration is water loss from leaves. This question is about the direction the shoot grows.
    ✘ photoreaction [sci.tropism.process-named-photosynthesis] — Not a term CCEA uses. The growth of a stem towards light is phototropism.
- 02 (Name the hormone): Which hormone causes a shoot to bend towards the light?
    ✔ auxin — Auxin. It is the only plant hormone CCEA's B1 names, so it is the only one that can be the answer here.
    ✘ insulin [sci.tropism.hormone-not-auxin] — Insulin is an animal hormone that lowers blood glucose. Summer 2025 examiners saw several animal hormones offered in this part.
    ✘ chlorophyll [sci.tropism.hormone-not-auxin] — Chlorophyll is the green pigment that absorbs light for photosynthesis, not a hormone.
    ✘ ADH [sci.tropism.hormone-not-auxin] — ADH is the human hormone that makes the kidney reabsorb more water. The plant hormone here is auxin.
- 03 (Get the direction of the uneven distribution right): A shoot is lit from the left only. Where does the auxin collect?
    ✔ on the right side, away from the light — More auxin where there is less light. Those cells then elongate more and the shoot leans left, towards the lamp.
    ✘ on the left side, facing the light [sci.tropism.auxin-on-lit-side] — That would make the lit side grow longer and the shoot would lean away from the lamp, which is not what happens. Auxin collects on the shaded side.
    ✘ at the tip only, never lower down [sci.tropism.auxin-on-lit-side] — Auxin is made at the tip but moves down the shoot. The bending happens below the tip, where the cells elongate.
    ✘ evenly all the way round [sci.tropism.auxin-on-lit-side] — Even auxin gives even growth and a straight shoot. One-sided light makes the distribution uneven — that is the whole mechanism.
- 04 (Elongation, not division): What does auxin do to the cells on the shaded side of a shoot?
    ✔ it makes them elongate — Elongate — they get longer. A row of longer cells on one side forces the shoot to curve.
    ✘ it makes them divide more often [sci.tropism.cells-divide-not-elongate] — More cells is cell division. The specification's word is elongation: the same cells become longer.
    ✘ it makes them photosynthesise faster [sci.tropism.cells-divide-not-elongate] — The shaded side receives less light, so it cannot be photosynthesising faster. Auxin acts on cell length.
    ✘ it makes them shrink [sci.tropism.cells-divide-not-elongate] — Shrinking on the shaded side would bend the shoot away from the light. Auxin lengthens cells.
- 05 (Say the consequence, not just the cause): The tip is cut off a young shoot, which is then lit from one side. What happens?
    ✔ it does not grow and does not bend — Both halves of the answer. Summer 2025 Higher examiners reported that most candidates said there was no tip or no auxin but fewer said there was no growth.
    ✘ it grows straight up but does not bend [sci.tropism.no-tip-no-growth-omitted] — The tip is the source of the auxin that makes cells elongate, so removing it stops the growth as well as the bending.
    ✘ it bends towards the light as usual [sci.tropism.no-tip-no-growth-omitted] — With no tip there is no auxin, so there is nothing to make one side elongate.
    ✘ it bends away from the light [sci.tropism.no-tip-no-growth-omitted] — Nothing makes a shoot bend away from light on this specification. With the tip gone, the shoot does neither.
- 06 (Give a direction, not an impression): Which of these is a full-mark answer to 'State what the shoot will do'?
    ✔ The shoot bends towards the light. — A direction and the thing it bends towards. That is what the mark scheme wants.
    ✘ The shoot looks for the light. [sci.tropism.plant-seeks-light] — A plant cannot look for anything. Describe the growth: the shoot bends towards the light.
    ✘ The shoot moves. [sci.tropism.direction-vague] — Too vague to score. Summer 2025 Foundation examiners described exactly this kind of answer. Say which way it bends.
    ✘ The shoot grows differently. [sci.tropism.direction-vague] — 'Differently' names no direction. Differential growth is the explanation; bending towards the light is the observation.

### ftm.science.b1.b1-plant-hormones-phototropism.01 [verified] sci.tropism.auxin-on-lit-side · earned P1,P2 · ccea-cer:science:2025-summer:B1H:Q4
STEM: Orla is asked: 'Explain how auxin causes a shoot to bend towards light coming from the left. [4]'
Her answer:
    1. Auxin is made at the tip of the shoot.
    2. The auxin moves down the shoot.
  ✘ 3. The light makes more auxin collect on the left side, facing the light.
    4. That side then makes more cells, so the shoot bends to the left.
  WRONG: Auxin collects on the side **away from** the light, not the side facing it. Line 3 has it the wrong way round, and line 4 then has to be wrong too: if the lit side grew longer, the shoot would lean **away** from the light. Line 4 also says **more cells**, when auxin causes the existing cells to **elongate**.
  FIX: Auxin is made at the tip of the shoot. / The auxin moves down the shoot. / The light makes more auxin collect on the right side, away from the light. / Auxin makes those cells elongate, so the right side grows longer and the shoot bends to the left, towards the light.
  FEEDBACK: Two marks from four, and the two you have are the two most people get. The fix is a single sentence to memorise: **more auxin where there is less light**. Check it against the picture every time — the long cells have to be on the outside of the curve, which is the shaded side. Then swap 'more cells' for 'elongate', because that is the word the scheme lists.
