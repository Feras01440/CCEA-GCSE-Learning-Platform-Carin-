### we.science.b2.b2-sex-hormones-menstrual-cycle.02 [verified] paper B2 calc
STEM: A woman's menstrual cycle lasts 31 days. Ovulation happens about 14 days before the end of a cycle. On about which day of her cycle does she ovulate? [2]
  1. Notice that the cycle is not 28 days long, so day 14 is not the answer.   — The fourteen days are counted back from the END of the cycle, not forwards from day 1. That is the whole idea being tested.  ()
  2. Count back: 31 − 14 = 17.   — Subtract the fourteen days from the length of the cycle.  (P1)
  3. So she ovulates on about day 17.   — Check it is sensible: a longer cycle should ovulate later than day 14, and 17 is later.  (P2)
  FINAL: About day 17.
  TWIN: A different woman's cycle lasts 26 days. On about which day does she ovulate?  → numeric 12 tol={"type":"absolute","value":0} forms=decimal

## DIAGNOSTICS
### dx.science.b2.b2-sex-hormones-menstrual-cycle.pre (pre) [verified]
- p1 (Recall the female reproductive system (previous topic)): Before we start, three things you have already met. Which organ releases an ovum?
    ✔ the ovary — The ovary. It makes ova and, as you are about to see, a hormone as well.
    ✘ the oviduct [sci.repro.ovary-oviduct-swapped] — The oviduct carries the ovum after it has been released.
    ✘ the uterus [sci.repro.ovary-oviduct-swapped] — The uterus is where an embryo would implant.
    ✘ the cervix [sci.repro.cervix-vagina-confused] — The cervix is the ring of muscle at the base of the uterus.
- p2 (Recall what a hormone is (B1)): What is a hormone?
    ✔ a chemical messenger carried in the blood — A chemical messenger, made by a gland and carried in the blood to the organ it affects.
    ✘ a type of cell — A hormone is a chemical, not a cell.
    ✘ a nerve impulse — Nerves carry impulses; hormones travel in the blood and act more slowly.
    ✘ an enzyme — Enzymes are catalysts. Hormones carry messages.
- p3 (Read a value off a line graph): A graph has days along the bottom and thickness up the side. Where do you read the thickness on day 12?
    ✔ go up from day 12 to the line, then straight across to the side axis — Up to the line, then across. That is the method every reading in this lesson uses.
    ✘ read the number 12 off the side axis — 12 is the day. The thickness is a different quantity on a different axis.
    ✘ read the highest point of the line — That would answer a different question — where the line peaks.
    ✘ find where the line crosses the bottom axis — That is where the thickness would be zero.

### dx.science.b2.b2-sex-hormones-menstrual-cycle (post) [verified]
- d1 (Give oestrogen's role): Which hormone repairs and thickens the lining of the uterus?
    ✔ oestrogen — Oestrogen builds the lining back up after menstruation.
    ✘ progesterone [sci.cycle.oestrogen-progesterone-roles-swapped] — Progesterone maintains the lining once it is thick.
    ✘ testosterone [sci.hormones.wrong-hormone-given] — Testosterone acts on male secondary sexual characteristics.
    ✘ ADH [sci.hormones.wrong-hormone-given] — ADH controls water in the kidney and belongs to B1.
- d2 (Give progesterone's role): Which hormone maintains the lining of the uterus in the second half of the cycle?
    ✔ progesterone — Progesterone keeps the lining thick and ready.
    ✘ oestrogen [sci.cycle.oestrogen-progesterone-roles-swapped] — Oestrogen built the lining; maintaining it is the other hormone's job.
    ✘ testosterone [sci.hormones.wrong-hormone-given] — Testosterone is made in the testes.
    ✘ auxin [sci.hormones.wrong-hormone-given] — Auxin is a plant hormone. It was one of the answers examiners saw on this question.
- d3 (Recall the day numbers): Between which days does menstruation usually occur in a 28-day cycle?
    ✔ days 1 to 5 — Days 1 to 5. Day 1 is the first day of bleeding.
    ✘ days 5 to 10 [sci.cycle.menstruation-days-wrong] — By day 5 menstruation is finishing and oestrogen is starting to rebuild the lining.
    ✘ days 14 to 19 [sci.cycle.menstruation-days-wrong] — Day 14 is ovulation, two weeks away from menstruation.
    ✘ days 23 to 28 [sci.cycle.menstruation-days-wrong] — At the end of the cycle the lining is still being maintained.
- d4 (Recall the day of ovulation): On which day of a 28-day cycle does ovulation usually happen?
    ✔ day 14 — About day 14, halfway through a 28-day cycle.
    ✘ day 1 [sci.cycle.ovulation-day-wrong] — Day 1 is the first day of menstruation.
    ✘ day 5 [sci.cycle.ovulation-day-wrong] — Day 5 is the end of menstruation.
    ✘ day 19 [sci.cycle.ovulation-day-wrong] — That counts fourteen days on from the end of menstruation. Count from day 1.
- d5 (Apply the rule to a cycle that is not 28 days): A woman's cycle lasts 31 days. Ovulation is about 14 days before the end of a cycle. On about which day does she ovulate?
    ✔ day 17 — 31 − 14 = 17. Count back from the end.
    ✘ day 14 [sci.cycle.ovulation-day-wrong] — Day 14 is right only for a 28-day cycle.
    ✘ day 15.5 [sci.cycle.ovulation-day-wrong] — That halves the cycle. Ovulation is a fixed number of days before the end, not at the midpoint.
    ✘ day 19 [sci.cycle.ovulation-day-wrong] — That counts fourteen days on from the end of menstruation.
- d6 (Say what happens after day 28): What happens to the lining of the uterus at the end of a cycle in which no fertilisation has happened?
    ✔ it breaks down and is lost — Progesterone falls, so the lining is no longer maintained and it breaks down.
    ✘ it thickens further [sci.cycle.lining-thickens-when-no-fertilisation] — It thickens in the first half of the cycle. At the end, without fertilisation, it is lost.
    ✘ it stays exactly as it is [sci.cycle.lining-thickens-when-no-fertilisation] — It stays thick only while progesterone keeps it there.
    ✘ it is absorbed back into the blood [sci.cycle.lining-thickens-when-no-fertilisation] — It is shed, which is what menstruation is.

### ftm.science.b2.b2-sex-hormones-menstrual-cycle.01 [verified] sci.cycle.ovulation-day-wrong · earned P1 · ccea-cer:science:2025-summer:B2H:Q2
STEM: Niamh is asked: 'A woman's menstrual cycle lasts 31 days. Ovulation happens about 14 days before the end of a cycle. Calculate the day she is likely to ovulate. [2]'
Her working:
    1. ovulation is about 14 days from the end
  ✘ 2. menstruation ends on day 5, so count on from there
    3. 5 + 14 = day 19
  WRONG: Line 1 quotes the rule correctly. Line 2 then counts from the wrong place: the 14 days are counted back from the END of the cycle, not on from the end of menstruation. The end of menstruation has nothing to do with it.
  FIX: ovulation is about 14 days from the end / the cycle is 31 days long, so count back from day 31 / 31 − 14 = day 17
  FEEDBACK: One mark of the two: the rule in line 1 is right, and a scheme that gives a mark for the method would credit it. A quick sanity check catches the rest — a cycle longer than 28 days must ovulate later than day 14, and day 19 is later than day 17 by more than the cycle is longer than 28.
