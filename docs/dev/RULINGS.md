# Rulings: the index

Every ruling that still binds Cairn's content, marking, checks and way of working, one line each: the date it was made, the rule in one sentence, where it is written or enforced (*binds*) and who made it (*from*). Gathered on 11 Oct 2026 from the lead's log of 7–11 Oct, `pipeline/prompts/author-topic.md`, the lead's handoff notes since 22 Sep and the engine's gate records (`scratchpad/state/engine/`, git-ignored). The programme assessment of 11 Oct (`docs/plan/review/2026-10-11-programme-assessment.md`, risk 8) asked for it: rulings were being lost or re-argued across sessions.

**How to use it.** The day a ruling is made, add its line here and write the rule into the file it binds (author-topic.md, STANDARDS.md, an engine file, a lint). A line whose *binds* says **log only** is a ruling not yet written into the file it governs: those are the next to write. The lead's log (`scratchpad/state/LEAD-LOG.md`) is history, not the rulebook. A superseded ruling stays listed with what replaced it, so nobody argues it again.

**Precedence.** The owner's rulings, then the lead's. On marking, the cited mark scheme decides: a ruling the scheme text contradicts is overturned by it (29 Sep 2026, below).

## 3. Marking: the scheme and the engine

- **25 Sep** (ruling 4) · A form task (simplify, factorise, expand, rationalise, a single fraction or logarithm, "express as", a recurring decimal) pays its matching common error, or nothing for the question typed back; a closing "in the form" on a multi-step part does not make it a form task. *Binds:* `src/components/items/mark.ts` (form tasks); author-topic.md "The cited scheme decides the marking". *From:* the lead.
- **25 Sep** (ruling 4), reaffirmed **11 Oct** · An instructed form is symmetric: a right value in the wrong form (a decimal where an exact value or a fraction is instructed, or the other way round) earns marks − 1, unless the stem demands working. *Binds:* `mark.ts`; the 0 an exact decimal earns today where a fraction is instructed is an engine fault owed to gate 3 (`scratchpad/briefs/2026-10-11/GATE-3.md`, item 2). *From:* the lead.
- **25 Sep** (ruling 4), confirmed **27 Sep** · A "show that" pays only through working: the printed result typed back earns nothing, and the part carries a working or steps spec, never a value spec. *Binds:* `mark.ts` (`workingOnly`); author-topic.md "The cited scheme decides the marking"; MARKING line in the build. *From:* the lead and the Fable judge.
- **25 Sep** (ruling 5) · A right answer written as a negation is paid: key words carry the positive idea and its negated forms, and a reject word is a wrong idea, never the fix for a negation. *Binds:* `src/components/items/text-marking.ts` (the negation model of 29 Sep). *From:* the lead.
- **25 Sep** (ruling 6), narrowed **27 Sep** · Name-only applies to the command word Name: such a stem pays the name and refuses a formula; Identify, What is, Give and Which accept either. *Binds:* author-topic.md "The cited scheme decides the marking". *From:* the lead and the Fable judge.
- **27 Sep** · A semicolon in a scheme separates points and a slash group is one point, so each key-word group is exactly one scheme point. *Binds:* author-topic.md "The cited scheme decides the marking". *From:* the Fable judge.
- **27 Sep** · `dependsOn` mirrors the cited scheme, never a general assumption. *Binds:* author-topic.md, same section. *From:* the Fable judge.
- **27 Sep** · `unitRequired` is set only where the scheme gives the unit its own mark, and never on a Maths or Further Maths part. *Binds:* author-topic.md, same section; MARKING line. *From:* the Fable judge.
- **27 Sep** · The form the stem asks for is held by the spec (`form`, `mustBeSimplified`, `acceptForms`), and `simplifiedOnly` is never used. *Binds:* author-topic.md, same section; MARKING line. *From:* the Fable judge.
- **27 Sep** · On a form task a common error earns at most marks − 1, valued from the cited scheme's lines. *Binds:* author-topic.md, same section; MARKING line. *From:* the Fable judge.
- **27 Sep** · A dependent part follows through by its relation, unless the cited scheme forbids it (then `noFollowThrough` quotes that line). *Binds:* author-topic.md, same section; MARKING line. *From:* the Fable judge.
- **27 Sep** · An order or steps part whose scheme prints thresholds will carry them once the engine's field lands; until then its log says so. *Binds:* author-topic.md, same section. *From:* the Fable judge.
- **27 Sep** (reversed for Further Maths) · In Maths and Further Maths a dropped final zero and more places than instructed are right, and the accuracy mark goes only for fewer places, or a decimal where an exact form is instructed; science marks instructed places as written. *Binds:* `mark.ts` (accuracy and `sciencePlacesDiffer`); kept by the C2 reviewer's ruling of 8 Oct. *From:* the lead.
- **29 Sep** (the lead's F) · Diagnosis is separate from marks: the question typed back earns nothing on any part, and a matching common error still names its misconception and feedback but pays nothing there. *Binds:* `mark.ts`. *From:* the lead.
- **29 Sep** (the lead's H) · A show-that verified by substitution earns the check's method marks, never the last mark; a common error that names a value as another mistake decides over the accuracy-only rule. *Binds:* `mark.ts`. *From:* the lead.
- **29 Sep** (the lead's I) · Off a show-that, a matched common error supplies the diagnosis and the tag but never raises the key words' award. *Binds:* `mark.ts` (kept at gate 2, G2-60). *From:* the lead.
