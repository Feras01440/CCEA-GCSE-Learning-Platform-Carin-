# Research application: what was adopted, what landed, what did not

Decision 6 of the platform programme (PLATFORM-PROGRAMME.md, 22 Sep 2026): "The research is applied, or the gap is named." Written by the research-plan agent, 22–23 Sep 2026.

## Method

One row for every adopt / adapt / reject decision in the quality bar's Part A (docs/plan/review/2026-09-19-quality-bar.md), for the ten changes in its Part C, and for every actionable recommendation in docs/research/04, 05, 06, 07 and 09. Each row carries the recommendation, its source, a verdict, the evidence in the code, and the concrete next task where the verdict is not "implemented".

Every evidence cell was found by grep and read on the source as it stood between 22 Sep 23:50 and 23 Sep 13:25 (clock verified with `date` at both ends). Counts over the content were produced by two read-only scripts kept in the research-plan agent's scratchpad (`depth-stats.mjs`, `data-counts.mjs`) and re-run at 23 Sep 13:20; the manifest held 176 topics at 22 Sep 23:51, 180 bundles on disk at 23:56 and 183 topics at 13:20 on 23 Sep, because twenty content agents were publishing throughout. The marking and item rows also draw on a sixteen-question read-only sweep by a child agent, filed word for word as docs/plan/review/2026-09-23-marking-evidence.md; every claim of that sweep that this table relies on was re-opened by the research-plan agent (Part H records the two the sweep itself flagged as overstated). Nothing in this table is assumed from a plan or a commit message. Paths are relative to the project root; a line number is the line as read on that date and may have moved since.

Verdicts: **Implemented** (the decision is true in the code and in the data), **Partial** (the mechanism exists but the promise is only partly kept, with what is missing named), **Not started**, **Rejected** (deliberately not done, with the reason). A recommendation that the platform declined for a reason the evidence supports is Rejected, not Not started.

Standing facts used by many rows (23 Sep 13:20): 183 published topics against a catalogue of 371 in her scope (maths Higher route M3 17/17, M4 9/9, M7 17/17, M8 15/15; Further Maths FM1 29/29, FM2 16/16, FM3 14/16; Double Award B1 21/21, B2 15/20, C1 2/25, C2 16/22, P1 2/22, P2 10/20; Unit 7 practical skills 0/4; Foundation maths M1/M2/M5/M6 0/94, not in her entries). Across 180 bundles: 3,596 question parts carrying 7,734 marks; 430 worked examples with 1,797 narrated steps, 430 twins and 430 faded plans; 1,452 diagnostic items in 281 sets; 96 insight cards with 458 examiner findings; 4,324 authored common errors on 2,843 parts (79%); 40,839 verification checks; 1,433 note gates; 863 retrieval prompts placed inside the notes; 154 videos and 38 simulations inside notes; 796 note figures; 2,741 flashcards in the built decks.
