# Trial audit: fm1/algebraic-fractions-simplify (measured on build 8, 25 Sep 2026)

Nine lenses read the trial topic on build 7 and build 8 of the static export (content truth, the marking engine on every item, code quality, learning design, desktop craft, phone craft, accessibility and motion, the Read v2 flow, the companion's rules), the findings were merged by area, and one adversarial verifier per area re-measured each finding on **build 8** (25 Sep, 01:08), sorting it into *confirmed* (still true on build 8 and a rule broken), *fixed* (true on build 7, resolved on build 8) or *refuted* (never a defect). The verifiers' evidence and scripts are under `scratchpad/platform/trial-audit/` (this session's scratchpad); the merged and verified data is `verified.json` there. The synthesis agent died at a usage wall; this document was assembled from the same data by the lead on 27 September.

## 1. The verdict, in five lines

1. The trial topic is not yet ready for the second sit-down with her: **91 findings** survived verification on build 8 (8 blockers, 49 majors, 34 minors); **40** more were real on build 7 and are fixed on build 8; 10 were refuted.
2. The open blockers are the marking engine on this topic (typing the question straight back earns every mark but one; right worked lines refused as "not the same line yet"), two accessibility failures in Slides (options with no accessible name; the verdict invisible to a screen reader), the hare missing from the Tonight tile after the first open, and four content and drawing items that the source has already fixed since build 8 (see §2's "moved" notes).
3. Since build 8 the source has moved further than the audit could see: the trial note was rewritten to teach → show → check with a two-sentence lede and corrected citations; the Slides drawings were rebuilt on the note's new worked example; the hero, the lede and the focus ring were fixed. Build 9 waits only for the fork's engine re-verification.
4. Nothing here needs a new decision from the owner: the eight teach-first answers (27 Sep) already settle the card grammar, the re-teach on a miss, the recall cards and the roll-out order.
5. Order of work: build 9 and the specs; then the Slides card grammar v3 and the open majors by area (Slides accessibility and phone, Read v2 state and flow, the companion's presence, Today); then FM1 roll-out as topics pass review; then the sit-down.
