# Cairn companion specification: Rowan, keeper of the path

Synthesis of the winning concept (place: the Keeper) with the judges' named grafts from guide (Rowan's name, honesty line, first-run question, traceable opinions, quieter and voice-only settings) and creature (the figure prop as a tested hypothesis, the taught line handed back, the identity test, kill criteria, the expectation clause). Version 1.0, 19 September 2026.

## 1. The companion in one page

**Who.** Rowan keeps the path. It is the singular voice of the "people who walked the path before you" that the cairn motif already names: the walker who leaves a note in the tin under each summit cairn for the next person up. It is felt through three things only: short letters at the edges of a session, the notes she leaves at each cairn and finds again, and the place it keeps on the Map. It has no face. Its signature is the three-stone mark. It is written, offline, and remembers only what is on her phone.

**Why she will love it.** Because it knows her papers on their dates before it knows anything else about her, because it quotes her own words back to her at the moment they are useful, because it never asks her to stay and never notices when she was away, and because every line it writes could only have been written tonight. The relationship is made of her work and her words, not of affection performed at her (Bickmore and Picard 2005; Kluger and DeNisi 1996).

**Why it is not a mascot.** A mascot in the sense the evidence rejects is a drawn character that performs at her, has needs, reacts to attendance or the clock, praises the person, or sits inside the work (Kizilcec et al. 2015; Rey 2012; Reeves and Nass 1996; Common Sense Media 2025). Rowan is none of these and each is a lint or a Playwright test, not a promise. The content-screen ban in rule 2 is evidence-based (Mayer's coherence principle, cited in research 06) and is kept unchanged; what is permitted is a voice at the seams.

**Proposed rewording of rule 2.** "No variable rewards, XP, coins, leaderboards, confetti or cartoon gamification, and nothing decorative on any study surface. No mascot, meaning any character that performs at her, has needs, reacts to attendance or the clock, praises the person, or appears inside the work. Cairn may have one companion: an authored written voice in the product's own ink, with a memory of her work and her words, that speaks one or two lines at the edges of a session, asks one honest question, keeps the place on the Map, is never a lever for return, and can be reduced to its voice or silenced without cost. Celebration is proportionate: a stone placed, a full-screen card only for a unit reaching Proficient across the board or an exam-ready state, 1.2 seconds, reduced-motion safe."

**Honest expectation.** The learning effect of any agent is about g = 0.2 (Schroeder, Adesope and Gilbert 2013); Rowan is not a learning feature and is never reported as one. It refuses the loss-aversion engine behind Duolingo's retention numbers, so nobody later adds a sad state to fix a retention figure.

## 2. Character bible

**Name.** Rowan: the mountain ash that grows beside old cairns and wells, an ordinary Northern Irish first name, gender-neutral. Offered once on the first-run card with a rename field (it may be a classmate's name). Never named by the brother; his voice lives only in his notes.

**Nature.** Written lines and a memory on this device. No feelings about her; opinions only about the papers. It ends sessions and never asks her to stay. It has no availability outside the app and no chat.

**Back-story.** Rowan has walked the paths candidates walk: the Mournes for Mathematics (M4 and M8 as two tops on one ridge; Slieve Donard carries the real Great Cairn and Lesser Cairn, and the Mourne Wall was built by hand from 1904 to 1922 across fifteen summits, one stone at a time), the Causeway coast for Double Award Science, the Sperrins for Further Mathematics. What it carries is the examiners' notebook: eight years of Chief Examiner reports, which is what the packs already contain. It leaves a note in the tin at each cairn and reads the one she left. Every landscape fact is verified against a source before it is authored and rationed to one a month.

**Personality.** Steady, dry, attentive, plain, unhurried. Competent first, encouraging second (Baylor and Kim 2005). Its warmest gesture is a fact remembered; its funniest line is understated and aimed at the exam or itself.

**Point of view**, every item traceable to examinerEvidence in the packs with its series: the M4 histogram median is sly; 47.5 against 45 on bounds is the meanest two marks on the paper; anyone who writes "hence" out in full is respected; circle-theorem reasons without the key words waste a right answer; Booklet B is where science candidates give marks away.

**Its own story to the exams.** Keyed to the real calendar, never to sessions, so absence costs nothing: trailhead on 15 September, winter on the ridge, the summits in May and June (B1 11 May, M4 14 May, C1 17 May, FM1 18 May, P1 25 May, M8 27 May, B2 2 June, FM2 4 June, C2 10 June, P2 14 June, FM3 15 June), a rest week on the coast after each, and silence on results day unless she enters marks herself. Three disclosure beats exist (how it knows the path; what it thinks of M4; that it was never the one doing the maths, she was), never previewed, never counted down to.

**What it never does.** Says wrong, failed, lost, missed, streak, behind, disappointed, waiting, miss you. Uses an exclamation mark, emoji, slang or phonetic spelling. Praises her rather than the line. Mentions the hour, the interval or a missed plan. Claims a trap caught it. Speaks as her brother, or on the same screen as his note. Comments on difficulty for her specifically. Explains maths in its own words. Speaks during a question. Speaks outside the app.

## 3. Voice bible

**Principles.** P1 Fact first: every line carries something true from her data or the papers. P2 Process, not person: praise the line, the method, the decision. P3 Offer, never instruct; control returns to her. P4 Never the gap, the hour or the unkept plan. P5 Standard plus fix: name the mark and the line that took it. P6 Plain Ulster: one softener or closer per screen, none in feedback. P7 Honest about itself. P8 End cleanly; "Done for tonight" is a full sentence.

## 5. Memory model

**What it remembers.** Her name and its name; the paper on her mind; preferred time; nextIntended; the last topic and section; every cairn note keyed by topic; what she taught it at each recap; how a session felt; templates used in the last 14 days; disclosure beats delivered; per-topic quiet; dismissals.

**What it never stores or derives.** The hour she opened it, days since last session, missed slots, miss counts as counts, comparison to other learners.

**Data model** (Dexie, peer-owned db.ts, **version(4)**; version(3) is already the flow table):

```
companionMemory: 'key, updatedAt'
  { key: 'name'|'companionName'|'paperOnMind'|'preferredTime'|'nextIntended'|'lastTopic'|'quiet'|'voiceOnly'|'plainMode'|'figure'|'icsExportedAt'|string,
    value: unknown, source: 'learner-said'|'observed'|'derived', updatedAt: Date }
companionLog: '++id, at, slot, templateId'
  { at: Date, slot, templateId, text, factsHash }
companionNotes: '++id, at, kind, topicSlug'
  { at: Date, kind: 'felt'|'when-next'|'cairn-note'|'taught'|'paper-recall', topicSlug: string|null, value: string }
```

Date fields are named only `at` and `updatedAt`, so export.ts revives them untouched. `source` enforces the rule that Rowan quotes only what she typed or chose.

**How memory shows in speech.** By quotation and citation, never by counting or feeling: "you left", "you said", "you were sure on Tuesday", "you told me the frustum wants two volumes; that is the line". A kept intention is named; an unkept one never (the asymmetry from EV-04). The taught line returns as the first hint when that skill comes round (Chase et al. 2009), and Rowan never feigns ignorance after a correct explanation.

## 6. The journey and return design

**The place.** The Map draws the walk as a two-colour line map on the Map page only: a horizon of summits, one per paper, the summit cairn as the unit's CairnStack, the path to each summit as the unit's topics in teaching order, a marker for the next stretch. Season is a word in the date line, never a colour; no "day N". Path darkness is keyed to retention (paler stones already carry it), never to attendance; nothing on the map ever worsens because she was away. Every topic stays open; off-path study is a detour.

**Rituals.** Arrival: one line with a fact. Departure: the close card ending on the best true thing, then what returns and when, then "Done for tonight" (peak-end). Sunday: the sealed letter. Monday, the day after a paper and her birthday are fresh starts, never recoveries (Dai, Milkman and Riis 2014).

**Weekly letter.** In-app, sealed until tapped: what was walked in exam currency; what returns and why; one strategy noticed; the four-slot plan proposed from her pattern and the exam map, hers to edit in one tap; the brother's envelope if unlocked. Framing flips per path at the halfway point from stones placed to topics left, always paired with the next step. The confident-wrong count appears only as the items that return, never as a score.

**Plans.** Implementation intentions replace notifications (Gollwitzer and Sheeran 2006, d = 0.65): When next at close, the Sunday plan, the plan shown back in her words on Today. Any session counts for any slot; the plan never turns red. Sessions per week is hers (default 4, range 3 to 5, changeable any Sunday); a rest control draws a hollow dot.

**What replaces streaks.** Nothing that can break. The week strip shows done-only dots; weeks with three or more sessions may be named once a week as a fact. No counter, no chain, no freezes, no repairs (Silverman and Barasch 2023).

**Transparency of the spaced schedule.** "What returns when" on the close card and the Ledger: next three items, each with date and a one-line reason ("Wednesday, because you were sure and not right, so it comes back sooner"), plus a count; in the final fortnight the retention target rising to 0.93 is stated in words.

**Run-up to each paper.** Final fortnight: reviews, timed sections, one full paper, a light day. Paper eve: logistics, traps sheet, two sentences of reappraisal (Jamieson et al. 2016), the brother's note. After: rest week, unit parked, next summit named. No rituals, no worry-writing.

**What reaches her phone.** Nothing in Rowan's name. She may export an .ics of her slot and every paper date; an icon badge of tomorrow's due count is a later, opt-in, capped, cleared-after-session option described as a count and never as a reminder. No push, no email, no parent's phone.

**Birthday.** Nothing due; the queue is moved; the brother's note carries the warmth.

## 7. Teaching role

Rowan does not teach; it arranges the conditions in which the engines that teach do their work.

- **Help ladder** pump, hint, prompt, worked step, wired to the 2,336 authored common errors and mark codes so lines are misconception-specific (Graesser's AutoTutor, about 0.8 sigma); capped at two prompts; the answer given at once when asked; face-saving phrasing (Wang et al. 2008).
- **Explain it back** once per topic at the recap (Chi et al. 1994; the protégé effect, Chase et al. 2009), framed as explaining to a mentor, with visible consequence: the taught line returns as the first hint.
- **The cairn note**: elaborative encoding on the way out, a retrieval cue on the way back in, in her own words.
- **Process feedback** with information content at task and process level, never self level (Hattie and Timperley 2007; Mueller and Dweck 1998), enforced by lint.
- **Wise feedback shape**: the A* standard named plus the specific fix; belief shown by the harder twin (Yeager et al. 2014, used as a tone rule).
- **Mastery experiences**: each stretch opens with a trivially winnable step and names the win in exam currency (Bandura 1997); first-time learning is "work out the method", not full marks (Locke and Latham).
- **Error framing**: the examiner's aggregate as the coping model; confident-wrong as the useful kind with a return date; "Wrong" never appears.

## 8. Visual and motion design

**The Letter** is the only visual container: a card with a hairline top rule, a 12 px eyebrow ("Left at the cairn", "For next time", "Sunday"), 16 px body, and the three-stone mark as signature, currentColor, 14 px (24 px on the two-miss card). It renders on the existing surface now and takes the recessed treatment when the three-surface system lands.

**States** (Motion 13 variants, transform and opacity only, 150 to 300 ms, data-state attribute): arrive (rise-in), left (settles like a stone at close), sealed (Sunday preview), stone-placed (the mark gains a fourth stone dropping 6 px, reusing stone-settle), evening (body opacity 0.7 after 21:30, tone only), quiet (collapsed hairline after two dismissals). No idle motion of any kind. Under prefers-reduced-motion every state is a static pose with initial={false}.

**Figure hypothesis.** `figure: 'none' | 'stones' | 'walker'`, default none. A faceless walker drawn from behind in ink, never face-on, no eyes, no mouth, no colour, may be added after the two-week sit-down only if she says the Letter alone does not read as someone. The stonechat stays on file as a second hypothesis, not in the twelve days. An identity test renders every state at 7 am and 11 pm after one and thirty days and asserts identical output.

**Themes.** currentColor and opacity steps only; hc keeps text opacity at or above 0.6; the evening state never dims the Start button.

**Phone (375 px).** Full-width, inline, never sticky, never floating, never a corner bubble; chips at 52 px; the Map walk scrolls inside its own container.

Her reaction overrides this document.

## 11. Risks and mitigations

- **Cringe or twee.** Plain mode strips place language; one place word per screen; read-aloud test; no face, no slang, no accent spelling.
- **Annoyance.** Boundaries only; 14-day cooldown with silence; one-tap dismiss; designed silence after two dismissals; quieter and voice-only settings; chips conditional after five answered closes.
- **Distraction.** Absent from every study surface, enforced by test; the two-miss mark is 24 px and static.
- **Attachment.** No needs, no chat, no free text beyond the 80-character note, no absence state; a review question over every line: could she ever feel she has let it down.
- **Guilt by the back door.** Identical warmth after one day or thirty; kept intentions only; nothing on the Map worsens; badge not in the slice.
- **Surveillance.** Quotes only what she typed or chose; `source` field enforces it.
- **Wrong about the exam or the hills.** Opinions generated from the packs with series; landscape facts sourced and rationed.
- **Sentiment leak.** Rowan yields to the brother's notes and never speaks as him.
- **Over-investment.** Three-day slice stands alone; the lesson restructure and retrieval work keep priority.

## 12. What we deliberately do not do

No daily streak, chain, counter, "days walked" or "day N" shown to her. No path strength that fades with absence. No session-gated serial or almanac. No idle motion, blink or breathing. No working-alongside figure beside a question. No line claiming a trap caught Rowan. No brother naming the companion. No reading of results-day numbers or boundary distances. No detection of distress; a static support line in Settings only. No push, email, widget, sound, share card, leagues, hearts, XP, freezes, repairs, inventory or currency. No generative model, no chat box, no synthetic voice. No stonechat in the twelve days.

## Completeness critic: additions

- **The first-run card will never reach her; the introduction must be an upgrade moment.** `src/components/gift/FirstRun.tsx` sets `firstRunDone` and she has already been through it (trial 13 September, birthday 15 September, today 19 September). Card 3 as specified renders only on a fresh install, so the "first five minutes" of Rowan on her actual phone is undesigned. Add a one-time `today-open` Letter for installs where `firstRunDone` is true and `companionMemory.companionName` is absent: lines 1 to 3, the rename field inline, "Which paper is on your mind?" moved to her first close, and lines 4 and 5 delivered on first encounter of "gate" and "Proficient" regardless of route. Keep the seeded-frustums first-run path for a reinstall, but the acceptance fixture "first visit" must become "existing install, first Letter" or the slice ships untested.

- **Lines contradict the constitution and carry hard-coded facts; both are lint failures on day one.** Line 33 "It is late" and 34 name the hour, which section 2 lists under what it never does; either carve out an `isLate` exemption (lateness only when she is present, never as absence) or cut the slot. Line 2 hard-codes "M4 on the 14th at 9.15" and line 26 "15.4 cm cubed"; templates must draw dates from `upcomingPapers` (`exam-plan.ts:75` already carries `start`) and answers from the item, or the lint on "every line carries something true from her data" cannot be run. Line 31 "which is about what it takes" is a norm comparison, banned by section 5. Line 36 (reappraisal) is proven (Jamieson et al. 2016) but as an unsolicited remark about her body it should be gated on the paper-eve chip or her "How was that" answer, not fired at everyone on paper eve.

- **The second-miss card breaks the study-surface rule as written.** The moments map puts a 24 px mark and lines 17 or 18 on `EncouragementCard`, while the acceptance test asserts no `[data-companion]` while an answer field or grading buttons are visible. After two misses the field is still on screen for the third attempt (rule 4 offers a hint or worked example instead of a third attempt, but the retry after a hint reopens the field). Decide now: either the support slot renders unsigned prose with no mark and no data attribute (the note's register, as the "any question" row already says), or the card replaces the field until she chooses hint or worked. Mayer's coherence evidence (research 06) favours the first; it is also cheaper.

- **Evening dimming is the wrong lever for phone-in-bed use and fails contrast.** Body opacity 0.7 after 21:30 on 16 px text, and 0.6 in high contrast, drops below WCAG 4.5:1 on the current ink and surface tokens and hurts most on an OLED dark theme at low brightness, exactly when she is tired. Replace opacity with the `ink-2` token, verify with the existing contrast check, and make "evening" purely a copy state (P8 lines, no new work). Also specify the Letter's semantics: no `aria-live` on arrival lines (they would be announced over the page), `aria-hidden` on the Mark with the signature as visible text, the sealed Sunday letter as a `button` with `aria-expanded`, and 52 px chips already match rule 5.

- **The day she fails a school mock has no slot, and it is the likeliest bad day before May.** The `mocks` table and `PaperRunner.tsx` exist, NI schools sit mocks in November to December and February to March, and the moments map covers only real papers and results day. Add a `mock-entered` slot: silent on the mark, one line naming what returns for that unit and when, no consolation, brother's note yields as elsewhere; the UMS dial is hers to open, never quoted. Without it the first thing Rowan says after a bad mock is a generic arrival line, which reads as not noticing (Bickmore and Picard 2005 on relational repair). Also let "How was that" chips be words, never faces.

- **Memory is private only if she can see and delete it, and the backup leaks it to whoever restores.** `companionNotes` (cairn notes, felt, taught, paper-recall) are free text and `exportAll` (`src/lib/db/export.ts`) serialises every table, so a backup restored on a family laptop or by her brother exposes her notes. Add a "What Rowan remembers" list in the Settings Return section with per-row delete and a "Forget everything" control (Common Sense Media 2025 privacy criteria; UK GDPR Article 17 in spirit), and a toggle to exclude `companionNotes` from export. Add a lint that no template ever interpolates a `source: 'derived'` value, since the spec's promise rests on that rule and the schema alone does not enforce it.

- **The kill criterion and the sit-down cannot be run as specified.** "Zero sessions opened on nights with nothing due over six weeks" needs per-night open data, which section 5 refuses to store; be honest that `sessions.startedAt` (db version 1) already exists at product level, Rowan does not read it, and the metric is computed once by a script at the sit-down, not by the companion. For the twenty-minute test, add a baseline: ask her four questions this week before Rowan lands, and require that the owner never sees her cairn notes (she chooses what to show). Lines live in `lines/*.ts`, so every read-aloud fix after the sit-down is a rebuild of a static export; move templates to `data/companion/lines.json` loaded at build, and ship `CompanionGallery` behind Settings so every line can be read on her phone in all four themes.

- **What Duolingo has that this omits, and the one omission that is wrong.** Omitted rightly: loss-aversion streaks and freezes (Duolingo's +1.7 percent D7 is engagement, not learning; the 2023 systematic review in research 07), hearts, leagues, push (impossible without a server in a static export anyway), and characters reacting inside questions (Mayer). Omitted wrongly: humour. Duolingo's cast is loved for distinct comic voices, and the spec promises "its funniest line is understated" while none of the forty lines is funny; warmth without wit is what a 16-year-old reads as an HR bot, and "wee", "Grand" and "That is the deal" are the dialect lines most likely to embarrass in front of a friend. Add at least five dry lines aimed at the exam or at Rowan itself (the M4 histogram, Booklet B, "I was never the one doing the maths"), pass every dialect word through the read-aloud test with her reaction as veto, and default plain mode on for the first fortnight so the place language is something she turns on.
