## 1. Book pages

Section B2, printed pp. 8–11 (PDF 10–13): introduction, safety and apparatus p. 8; set-up drawing, fifteen-step method and results table p. 9; the printed equation, a working space and Questions 1–3 p. 10; Questions 4–5 p. 11.

## 2. The book's method, in order (our words)

1. Food carries energy; burning a weighed sample under water and measuring the warming of the water is how it is compared. The sample is a mini cracker. Expected result: the water warms (p. 8).
2. Fix a clamp to a stand with a boss and clamp a boiling tube tilted away from upright (p. 9).
3. Weigh the cracker to one decimal place and record it (p. 9).
4. Measure 20 cm³ of water in a 25 cm³ measuring cylinder into the tube; read and record the starting temperature (p. 9).
5. Put the cracker on a mounted needle (or in a wire-mesh dish on a tripod and gauze, lit with tongs), light it in the Bunsen flame and hold it under the tube until it has burnt away completely; if it goes out, light it again and put it back (p. 9).
6. Stir, read the temperature at once, record it, and subtract to get the rise (p. 9).
7. Energy per gram from the printed one-line equation: rise × volume of water × 4.2, all divided by the mass of food (p. 10).

## 3. Where the book is better than our note

- **CCEA's own equation layout.** The book prints energy per gram as a single fraction with the **volume** of water in cm³ in the numerator (p. 10), exactly as the 2023 paper did. The b1 note teaches two lines with the mass of water; u7-carrying-out already uses CCEA's one-line form. Both are correct; the b1 note should show CCEA's layout once so she recognises it on the paper.
- **Predict the effect of five changes** (p. 11, Question 4). Five rows, three columns: increase, decrease, no effect. CCEA's 2023 Higher Booklet A Q1(f) is this table for five marks (one per row), answered: more water, decrease; food held closer, increase; incomplete burning, decrease; a lower starting temperature, **no effect**; a bigger piece of food, increase. The b1 note teaches none of it; u7-carrying-out covers three rows (closer, more water, colder start) in one gate and its option notes. The "no effect" row is the one that tests understanding: the rise, not the final temperature, is what is measured.
- **What percentage of the packet's value heated the water** (p. 11, Question 5): the measured value over the stated value, times 100, to the nearest whole number. On the book's own figures, 2940 ÷ 12 800 × 100 = 22.97, so 23 %. CCEA's 2023 Higher Q1(g) asked the same for 3 marks (230.7 ÷ 330.6 × 100 = 69.78, so 70 %); the report says a few candidates found the percentage **lost** (30 %) and earned one mark of three. The b1 note lacks this; u7-carrying-out has it (worked example 01, find-the-mistake 01).
- **Stirring is about accuracy, repeats about reliability** (p. 10, Questions 1–2). CCEA's 2023 Foundation scheme: stirring spreads the heat so the water is the same temperature throughout (the report: an answer had to mention heat, temperature, hot or cold water); repeats give more reliable results, allow a mean and show up anomalies (the report: weaker candidates wrote "more accurate").
- **Safety after an accident** (p. 10, Question 3). A table of hazard, what happened, what to do. CCEA 2023 Foundation Q1(i): broken measuring cylinder, any two actions for 2 marks (informing the teacher, binning the broken glass, mopping up the spill); a burn, cool it under the cold tap for several minutes. The report names "tell someone" and "keep away from it" as the vague answers of weaker candidates. Our notes give hazards with controls but never the response to an accident.
- **Relight if it goes out**, and weigh to 1 d.p. (p. 9). u7-carrying-out has both; the b1 note has neither.

## 4. Where the book is worse, and what it leaves out

- **No reason why the value is low.** The book only implies it through Question 5. Our b1 note's paragraph (heat escapes to air and glass; incomplete burning; accuracy, not reliability; a shield or holding the food closer raises the value) is the explanation CCEA's six-markers want (2026 Higher Booklet B Q2 asks for two reasons).
- **No meaning for 4.2** and no reason why the volume can stand in for the mass. Our b1 note explains both.
- **The drawing is unlabelled** (p. 9). It would not earn an apparatus-diagram mark; ours is labelled in words.
- **Safety lines are generic** ("take care when..."), the kind of answer the 2023 report called vague.
- **No proportionality.** Our b1 note's graph of rise against mass of food (straight, through the origin) and our bundle item 0007 have no counterpart in the book.

## 6. Calculations and data tasks

- **Rise in temperature**: final minus initial (p. 9).
- **Energy per gram** (p. 10): rise × volume × 4.2 ÷ mass. CCEA 2023 scheme: one mark for rise × 20 × 4.2, one for dividing by the mass, one for the answer on her own data.
- **Percentage of the stated value** (p. 11): 23 % on the book's figures; 70 % on CCEA's 2023 figures. A second route for both: 2940 × 100 = 294 000, ÷ 12 800 = 22.97; 230.7 × 100 = 23 070, ÷ 330.6 = 69.78. (The 2023 scheme prints 69.7 as its middle line, a truncation; the rounded answer, 70, is right either way.)

## 7. The book's questions, and what CCEA's schemes credit

| Book question (page) | Scheme answer and source |
|---|---|
| 1: how stirring improved accuracy (p. 10) | Spreads the heat evenly / water the same temperature throughout (2023 F BkA Q1(f)(ii)). |
| 2: why repeat (p. 10) | Any two: more reliable; a mean can be found; anomalies can be spotted (2023 F BkA Q1(g)). |
| 3: safety table (p. 10) | Broken glass: two actions, such as informing the teacher, binning the glass and clearing the water (2 marks); hot apparatus: the event is a burn from touching it (1 mark) (2023 F BkA Q1(i)). |
| 4: effect of five changes (p. 11) | Decrease, increase, decrease, no effect, increase, one mark a row (2023 H BkA Q1(f)). |
| 5: percentage of the stated value (p. 11) | Measured ÷ stated × 100, rounded as told (2023 H BkA Q1(g)). |

For the six-mark method (2021 Booklet B, both tiers), the scheme's top band asks for a single safety point, two or more measurements (mass of food, starting and final temperature, volume of water) and at least one controlled variable (volume of water, distance of the food from the tube, food burnt completely). The book's method contains the measurements and two of the controls (the volume of water; burning completely); it never fixes the distance of the food from the tube.

## 8. Definitions and wording

- **Book slips, not to copy:** "Volume or water" for volume of water and "J/per gram" in the equation and answer line (p. 10); "Take care when with using" (p. 8). The units on the page read J per gram, which is right.
- **The tilted tube.** The book never gives a reason; do not invent one in a model answer.

## 9. Higher-tier-only content

None in the book. In 2023 CCEA set the full five-row table and the percentage question on the Higher paper only; the Foundation paper asked one row of the same idea (doubling the water to 40 cm³: decrease, F BkA Q1(h)).

## 10. Pitfalls and precautions the book builds in

- Burn the sample **completely**, relighting it if needed (p. 9).
- **Stir** before the final reading, and read it **straight away** (p. 9).
- Weigh to **one decimal place**, the place the table asks for (p. 9).

## 11. GAPS in our note, ranked

1. **The five-change table is missing from the b1 note.** Add a Your turn with five choice rows, each option note giving the reason (more water shares the same energy; closer loses less heat; unburnt food releases less; the starting temperature changes the final value but not the rise; more food releases more). The 2023 Higher shape, five marks.
2. **No percentage-of-the-packet task in the b1 note.** Add a See it on our own numbers (for example 3150 J/g measured against 18 000 J/g stated: 3150 ÷ 18 000 × 100 = 17.5 %) and a twin; give "the percentage lost" an option note from the 2023 report.
3. **Stirring and repeats in examiners' words.** Two gates: stirring, heat spread evenly; repeats, reliable, a mean, anomalies. The "more accurate" option gets the 2023 report as its note.
4. **Safety after an accident.** One line in u7-carrying-out (or the b1 note): tell the teacher, not "someone"; cold running water on a burn for a few minutes; broken glass into the glass bin.
5. **Show CCEA's one-line equation in the b1 note** beside our two lines, and add "relight it if it goes out" and "weigh to one decimal place" to its method paragraph.

## 12. Anything in our note the book suggests is wrong or misleading

Nothing. The b1 note's "take the highest temperature reached" and the book's "stir and read at once" lead to the same reading in practice; CCEA's 2023 instruction was the book's.
